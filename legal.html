The optional AI risk assessment

Only if you click "Analyze with Gemini" (and in the follow-up chat), the pasted text, the locally computed metrics and your chat messages are sent to this site's serverless function (hosted by Netlify) and forwarded via Netlify's AI Gateway to Google's Gemini API, where they are processed to generate the assessment. The operator does not store the submitted texts or the generated assessments; the site keeps no accounts and no database.
